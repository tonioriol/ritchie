Personal Google Workspace CLI

A private command-line integration operated by Toni Oriol for his own Google account. It is not a public service and does not offer sign-up or access to other users.

Purpose

The integration supports owner-directed local automation with Gmail, Google Sheets, and Google Search Console. Depending on the authorization granted and the task requested, it can read or manage email, read or update spreadsheets, and access Search Console information for the owner's sites.

Google authorization is used to access the owner's Google data. This public website only describes the integration; it does not collect Google credentials or display Google account data.
