Google data accessed and used

Depending on granted permissions and the owner's instructions, the integration may access Gmail messages and mailbox settings; Google Sheets spreadsheet content; and Google Search Console site and performance information. Access is used to carry out the owner's requested email, spreadsheet, and site-management tasks. Granted permissions do not by themselves authorize every possible action.

Storage and disclosure

Authorization credentials, local caches, and task outputs may be stored on the owner's devices. Data also remains in the relevant Google services, and requested updates are sent to those services. Google data and credentials are not published on this website or offered for sale. This integration is not a mechanism for sharing the owner's private Google data with other users.

This description does not promise that every local copy is automatically deleted after a task. Local task outputs and backups are managed by the owner. Any separate tool or service used for a task is subject to the owner's selection and authorization; this policy does not make claims about those services' independent practices.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop using the integration, remove its access through the Google Account's third-party connections settings, and delete local credentials, caches, and outputs. Revoking access prevents future authorized API access but does not automatically delete prior local outputs or data stored in Google services.

The hosting provider may process ordinary HTTP request information, such as IP addresses and requested URLs, to deliver and protect the website. Private Google account data is not part of these pages.
